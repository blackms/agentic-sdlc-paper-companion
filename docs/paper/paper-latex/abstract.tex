Procedural protocols deliver +0.59 to +0.78 expected-utility gain over free prompting at log-token ratios 0.70--3.28 on 8 cells $\times$ 4 frontier models. However, 33--68\% of procedurally-compliant runs still fail held-out semantic tests, exposing a conformance-without-semantics regime invisible to compliance-only metrics. We present a measurement framework that projects agent trajectories onto a behavior signature $B = \varphi(\tau)$ over conformance, target-correctness, robustness, and token cost, with a decisional cost-aware metric $\Vmetric$ admitting a closed-form break-even $\lambda_{\mathrm{breakeven}}$ below which the protocol pays.

\textbf{E1} validates empirical FOSD on the held-out utility grid ($n = 120$, all $80$ thresholds; sample-level, not population). \textbf{L1} (asymmetric-quorum joint-miss bound, inclusion--exclusion arithmetic) is validated within the finance contract domain ($\bar{\rho}: 0.62 \to 0.29$, joint-miss $22.91\% \to 12.28\%$, paired McNemar $p = 0.0013$ on $n = 200$ bugs $\times$ $1{,}400$ reviews); Phase-5 falsifies cross-domain transfer (\texttt{bankcheck.py}, $p \ge 0.5$ in all $5$ contrasts). \textbf{C-T3} converges in simulation but the LLM-grounded counterpart shows no improvement under two pre-registered injection formats (abstract-name and concrete-example, $p \in [0.95, 0.97]$ against $H_1$ at $n=92$), a simulation-to-LLM gap reported as absence of detected improvement, not evidence of absence. \textbf{C1} (contract-aligned specificity) replicates across $3$ reviewer families on $4$ phases; Phase-11 provenance-label falsification ($n=60$, both pre-registered hypotheses fail at $p \in [0.13, 0.34]$) rules out anti-stdlib carelessness as the dominant mechanism, leaving $\ge 40$ pp of the cross-sample detection gap to code-intrinsic factors. A naturalistic Phase-12 replication on CPython \texttt{csv.py} bugs harvested from the issue tracker is underpowered at the harvested sample size ($n = 8$, below the pre-registered target of $25$--$30$); $0/3$ families reach the Bonferroni-corrected primary threshold (Codex $p = 0.029$, Gemini $p = 0.031$, Opus $p = 0.50$) and Codex output is contaminated by $100\%$ CPython issue-number leakage in the out-of-band probe---naturalistic C1 transfer at low $n$ with the leakage caveat is therefore \emph{reported but not claimed}.

The contribution is methodological discipline---explicit separation of \emph{proved}, \emph{measured}, \emph{conjectured}, and \emph{empirically falsified}---not a new prompting technique. Every numerical claim recomputes from raw artefacts in the companion repository.
